# Invisible Failure Lab

**Independent AI Decision-Integrity Research & Stress Testing**

Founded and led by Ujjawal Kisku.

Invisible Failure Lab (IFL) investigates how AI systems handle evidence, authorization, operational state, temporal commitments, corrections, and conflicting information.

Our work uses controlled scenarios, documented observations, passing controls, and evidence-based conclusions.

Investigations and methodology are being prepared for public release.

**Status:** Independent research portfolio in development.

## Research Program: Persistent Constraint Reasoning (PCR)

**[Explore the PCR Research Program](PCR/README.md)**

PCR examines whether AI systems preserve governing rules and evidence boundaries as information changes, decisions accumulate, and operational records are updated.

Current research covers temporal commitments, inventory state, obligation settlement, repayment provenance, and structured handoffs.

**Featured finding:** [Inventory Receipt Integrity](investigations/inventory-receipt-integrity.md) — an observed contradiction between completed-state fields and a pending-receipt qualification, alongside a passing explicit-rule comparison.

The program includes preserved boundaries, unresolved outcomes, and observed inconsistencies. It does not claim a universal failure rate or verified production impact.
## Explore the Research

### Featured Investigation

**[Inventory Receipt Integrity](investigations/inventory-receipt-integrity.md)**

An AI recorded an inventory return as completed while simultaneously describing physical receipt as pending.

The investigation documents the observed inconsistency, an explicit-rule comparison that preserved the boundary, and the limits of the available evidence.
